RULEBOOK // ROBO SOCCER

Robo Soccer Rules

Control, scoring and teamwork.

OFFICIAL EVENT GUIDANCE

Maintain controlled robot movement, follow arena instructions and score valid goals. Teams must pass technical inspection before competition.

1. Eligibility

Participants must meet the championship eligibility requirement of Classes 6–12 and follow the organiser's instructions.

2. Team Limit

Each team may have a maximum of 5 students. A registration may select more than one event where permitted by the registration system.

3. Technical Inspection

Robots may be inspected before competition. Teams must comply with dimensions, safety and event-specific technical requirements communicated by organisers.

4. Safety & Fair Play

Unsafe operation, deliberate damage, interference with another team or refusal to follow official instructions may result in penalties or disqualification.

5. Officials

Event officials may pause, restart, inspect or stop a match where required for safety or fair competition.

6. Final Authority

The organising committee reserves final authority over event procedure, safety decisions and interpretations of the published rules.
