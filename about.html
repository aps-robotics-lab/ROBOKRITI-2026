ROBOKRITI 2026 // ABOUT

WHERE IDEAS BECOME MACHINES.

RoboKriti is the robotics championship of APS LBS Marg Tinkering Lab, created to turn classroom curiosity into practical engineering and healthy competition.

THE MISSION

LEARN BY BUILDING.

Participants move through a complete engineering cycle: register, design, build, test and compete. Every event rewards a different combination of technical thinking, control, strategy and teamwork.

The championship is designed around four principles: engineering, innovation, creativity and competition.
