THE CHAMPIONSHIP

ABOUT
ROBOKRITI.

APS Tinkering Lab presents a student-focused robotics championship built around engineering, innovation, creativity and competition.

01

THE TINKERING LAB

A space for students to experiment, prototype, test ideas and learn through making. RoboKriti carries that maker mindset into a live arena.

02

THE CHAMPIONSHIP

RoboKriti 2026 takes place on 02 September 2026 at Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow. Entry is free and teams can have up to five members.

03

THE MISSION

Encourage disciplined problem-solving, teamwork and practical engineering through competition while giving students a stage to build something real.

04

THE FORMAT

Four arenas — Robo Soccer, Robo Tug of War, Robo Race and Robo War — each testing a different engineering instinct.
