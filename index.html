01 / ABOUT

Where young minds
engineer the future.

ROBOKRITI 2026 is the robotics championship of Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow. It brings students together to design, build, program and compete with robots in exciting engineering challenges.

The championship is designed around creativity, problem-solving, teamwork, precision and responsible engineering.
