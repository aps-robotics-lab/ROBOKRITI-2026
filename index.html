01 // THE CHAMPIONSHIP

WHERE IDEAS BECOME MACHINES.

RoboKriti 2026 brings APS LBS Marg students into a single arena to design, build, test and compete. The championship celebrates practical engineering, disciplined teamwork and the courage to iterate.

From speed and control to strength and tactics, every arena asks teams to solve a different engineering problem under competition pressure.
