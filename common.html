Rules

Common Rules

Rules applicable to RoboKriti 2026 participants.

Team & Eligibility

Teams must satisfy the championship eligibility and team-size requirements.

Robot Safety

Robots must be constructed and operated safely. Organizer safety instructions must be followed.

Competition Conduct

Fair play, respect for opponents and compliance with referee decisions are required.

Equipment

Teams are responsible for required equipment and event readiness. Organizers may inspect robots.

Disqualification

Rule violations, unsafe behavior, deliberate interference or failure to follow organizer instructions may lead to disqualification.

Organizer Authority

Organizers and referees may make decisions necessary for fair competition and safety.

Event-specific numerical specifications, scoring and match conditions will be published/updated by the championship organizers before competition.
